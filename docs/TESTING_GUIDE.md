# NetraID, How to Use the App

## Install

Install `NetraID-universal.apk`. Android will warn that it is from an unknown
source, since it is not on the Play Store. Allow it.

On first launch, **grant the camera permission**. Nothing else is requested.

## Enroll a person

Open **Enroll Personnel**.

**1. Get the ring green.** Hold the phone about 40 cm away, face inside the ring.

| Ring | Meaning |
|---|---|
| White | No face, or too far away |
| Amber | Move closer, centre your face |
| Green | Good, hold still |

**2. Hold steady for six shots.** Six dots sit below the ring and fill one by one.
The counter at the top right goes from `0/6 CAPTURED` to `6/6`.

Keep still and keep looking at the camera. A dot appears only when the frame is
sharp, well lit and front facing, so some frames are skipped. **This is normal.**
Hold steady and the dots fill.

**3. Enter the name, then save.** The Save button stays locked until all six dots
are filled. Type the personnel ID, then press **Save Template**.

## Verify

Open **Verify Attendance**.

**1. Follow the two prompts.** The app asks for two actions at random, from blink,
smile, turn left, turn right. Do each one **as soon as it appears**.

If an action comes too late, the app starts over with two new ones. **This is
normal.** It is a reaction test. Follow the next prompt promptly.

**2. Hold still for three captures.** The card reads *Gestures verified, checking*
and counts `capturing 1/3`, `2/3`, `3/3`.

Look straight at the camera. If the last action turned your head, the app waits
until you face it again before capturing. **This is normal.**

**3. Read the result.** The card shows the matched personnel ID and a score. A
genuine match usually scores between 0.65 and 0.86.

## Normal behaviour

| What you see | Why |
|---|---|
| Dots fill slowly | Only sharp, well lit, front facing frames are kept |
| Prompts start over | An action came too late. Follow the next one promptly |
| Pause after a head turn | It waits until you face the camera before capturing |
| Save button locked | All six shots must be captured first |
| Camera is black | Camera permission was declined. Grant it in Android settings |

## Try it offline

Turn off mobile data and Wi-Fi completely, then enroll and verify. Everything
works the same. Nothing about authentication uses the network.
